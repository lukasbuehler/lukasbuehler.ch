For my master’s thesis at ASL, ETH Zürich, I designed a relative imitation-learning approach using egocentric vision and relative task-space velocities.

I demonstrated zero-shot perceptual and kinematic transfer from a seven-degree-of-freedom robotic arm to a fully actuated UAV.

I also implemented and evaluated Temporal Striding for ACT to improve compute efficiency and stabilise high-frequency velocity learning.
