Aegis started with a question about [[notes/looking-for-signals|AI safety and internal representations]]. Could an autoencoder help identify signals associated with scheming in a small language model?

I’ve started exploring the idea. The approach is more complicated than the initial question suggests; this is an investigation, with no claim of a reliable detector yet.

The project and its notes will develop together as the question becomes more precise.
