Recently I watched of [Theo t3.gg's YouTube video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GtJKFhztgdM),
where he described his role in Software also changing. He described it becoming more and more like Gardening.
His repo is like a big garden and he sets it up for success, the right tests, actions, agent and team instructions, he watches over pull requests, and watches for weeds or errors springing up. 
That makes a lot of sense to me. With agents, code bases are becoming less deterministic, as there won't be this one person that knows everything anymore. But I think that was just a feature of Software, the larger it grew, especially with teams the more thought has to go into keeping it modular and managing who does what.
I agree with his analogy. But I don't want to be a gardener.

I want to build, create, engineer, and solve the puzzles inside the larger task. So if Software is solved, and the puzzles are solved, where do I go?
I want to solve hard problems, so I will go looking for them.
I will go where the problems are plentiful and solving them is rewarding. For me, that is robotics. I want to tackle Sim-to-Real problems, engineering problems, Deep learning problems etc. I leave the algorithmic optimization to the coding agents.

"But Lukas", you might say, AI is going to solve that too. Yeah, probably. AI is great at solving known problems. And it also started solving unknown or research problems now. (Source: millenium problem openai and antrhopic ETH)
I just think there is a lot for me to do in Robotics and a lot that excites me.

Maybe it's a part of growing up.
Coding is dead. Long live Software Engineering. And Robotics.
